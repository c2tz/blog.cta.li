The User-Agent requests static WOFF2 faces with genuine 400 and 700 weights.
Google Fonts provides the Latin and Latin Extended subsets. These files are unmodified.
Both subsets are loaded together to cover French accents, including uppercase Y diaeresis.
The build converts WOFF2 to TTF in memory for Satori; visitors receive the generated PNG.
